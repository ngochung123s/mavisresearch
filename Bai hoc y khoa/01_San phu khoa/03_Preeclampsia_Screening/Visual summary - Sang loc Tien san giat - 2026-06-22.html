1. ISSHP 2018 - Dinh nghia Preeclampsia

Tieu chi bat buoc

HA >= 140/90 mmHg (it nhat 2 lan, cach nhau >= 4 gio) SAU 20 tuan thai ky o phu nu truoc do binh thuong.

+ 1 trong 3 tieu chi

1. Proteinuria (P/C ratio >= 30 mg/mmol)
2. Roi loan co quan (than, gan, than kinh, huyet hoc)
3. Roi loan nhau thai (FGR, abnormal UA Doppler)

FIGO 2019 phan loai: Early-onset (<34w) / Preterm (<37w) / Late-onset (>=34w) / Term (>=37w). Muc tieu chinh cua screening la PRETERM PE.

3. So sanh 3 phuong phap sang loc PE

Chaemsaithong 2022 (AJOG PMID 32682859).

Phuong phap nao tot nhat?

  • • ACOG 2013: chi 5% DR - kem
  • • NICE 2019: 41% DR - trung binh
  • • FMF Triple Test: 75-90% DR - tot nhat hien nay
  • • NNT = 250 (sang loc 250 de phong 1 ca preterm PE)

4. ASPRE MAIN RCT (NEJM 2017, n=1776)

Aspirin 150 mg/dem vs Placebo, 11-14 -> 36 tuan.

ASPRE (Rolnik 2017, PMID 28657417)

  • • Preterm PE: 1.6% aspirin vs 4.3% placebo
  • • OR 0.38 (95% CI 0.20-0.74), p = 0.004
  • • Reduction 62%
  • • Adherence: 79.9% uong >= 85% lieu

5. FMF Triple Test - 4 thanh phan

1. Maternal risk factors

Tuoi, BMI, chung toc, parity, tien su PE, tien su gia dinh, benh nen (HTN, DM, SLE, APS, than), ART, hut thuoc.

2. MAP (Mean Arterial Pressure)

Do 2 lan, moi ben canh tay, nghi 5 phut. PE som: MAP TANG (dau hieu som nhat).

3. PlGF (Placental Growth Factor)

Mau me, do ELISA. PE som: PlGF GIAM (< 0.7-0.8 MoM). Day la marker nhay nhat.

4. UtA-PI (Uterine Artery)

Doppler qua ca 2 dong mach tu cung. PE som: UtA-PI TANG + notching som.
