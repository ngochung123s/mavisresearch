Tu cung nhi hoa (Hypoplastic Uterus)

OB-GYN Mullerian Anomaly ASRM Class I ESHRE-ESGE U1 Uterus Transplant

1. Dinh nghia va phan loai

Tieu chan chan doan ASRM 2021

- Buong tu cung < 5-6 cm
- HOAC than tu cung < 4 cm
- Ty le than/co < 1:1 (binh thuong 2:1)
- The tich buong < 2.5-3 mL

Phan loai 2 guideline

ASRM 2021: Class Ia (Mullerian agenesis/hypoplasia segment)
ESHRE-ESGE 2013: U1 (dysmorphic uterus)

2 he thong deu cong nhan la 1 thuc the rieng biet, khac voi septate (ASRM II/ESHRE U2) va bicornuate (ASRM III/ESHRE U3).

7. 5 nhom nguyen nhan gay tu cung nhi hoa

1. Bam sinh - ong Muller

Loi hop nhat/thoai trien mot phan ong Muller. Hoi chung MRKH, gene LHX1, HNF1B, TBX6.

2. Bam sinh - tin hieu estrogen

Khiem khuyet ERalpha, aromatase deficiency. Estrogen khong the kich thich tang truong tu cung.

3. Mac phai - phoi nhiem DES

DES trong tu cung (me dung DES) -> tu cung hinh T, nhi hoa, voi tu cung bat thuong.

4. Mac phai - thieu estrogen

POI (suy buong trung som), chan an tam than, hoa tri/xa tri vung chau truoc day thi.

5. Di truyen - gene

MRKH type II lien quan 17q12 (LHX1, HNF1B), 16p11.2 (TBX6), GREB1L, PAX8. Di truyen RAT KHONG DONG NHAT.
