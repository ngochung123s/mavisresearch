Fetal US Doppler FGR Anemia thai Preeclampsia

1. Ba mach mau cot loi trong Doppler thai ky

Umbilical Artery (UA)

Phan anh suc can tuan hoan nhau-nhau (uteroplacental + fetoplacental). Tieu chuan vang cho FGR.

Middle Cerebral Artery (MCA)

Phan anh su phan phoi mau len nao ("brain-sparing effect"). MCA-PSV >= 1.5 MoM = anemia thai.

Ductus Venosus (DV)

Phan anh chuc nang tim thai va tinh trang toan. a-wave reversed = can thiep nang.

3. Ba chi so Doppler co ban

PI (Pulsatility Index) - Gosling

PI = (PSV - EDV) / TAV

Pho bien nhat. PI tang = suc can tang. PI giam = suc can giam.

RI (Resistance Index) - Pourcelot

RI = (PSV - EDV) / PSV

It dung hon PI, thuong dung cho Doppler than.

PSV (Peak Systolic Velocity)

PSV (cm/s)

Dung cho MCA-PSV (anemia). Nguong: >= 1.5 MoM = nghi anemia.

9. Khi nao dung Doppler nao?

Nghi ngo FGR (EFW < p10)

UA serial -> MCA, CPR

FGR som (< 32 tuan)

UA -> DV (giup timing can thiep)

FGR muon (>= 32 tuan)

UA -> MCA, CPR (brain-sparing)

Thai anemia

MCA-PSV (>= 1.5 MoM = nghi anemia)

Preeclampsia screening

Uterine artery quy 1 + PAPP-A, PlGF

Twin pregnancy

UA ca 2 + MCA ca 2 + DV cho TTTS
