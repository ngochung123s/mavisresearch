1. 3 muc tieu FTS (11+0 den 13+6 tuan)

1. Dating

Xac dinh tuoi thai chinh xac qua CRL (chinh xac +/- 5-7 ngay). Khi CRL > 84 mm chuyen sang HC.

2. Aneuploidy screening

Combined test (NT + PAPP-A + free beta-hCG) hoac NIPT (cfDNA). Detect T21 ~90-99%.

3. Early anatomy

Phat hien ~50% di tat lon. Cau truc: nao, tim, bung, cot song, tu chi, day ron, placenta.

3. NIPT performance (Zhang 2015, n=146,958)

Clinical experience BGI - 146,958 pregnancies, khong khac biet high-risk vs low-risk.

NIPT (cfDNA) - Zhang 2015

  • • Sensitivity T21: 99.17%
  • • Sensitivity T18: 98.24%
  • • Sensitivity T13: 100%
  • • Specificity: >99.95% ca 3 trisomy
  • • PPV phu thuoc a priori risk (low-risk co PPV thap hon)

4. Combined test - Pattern dac trung

PAPP-A va free beta-hCG giup phan biet T21 vs T18 vs T13 som.

Pattern biochem

  • • T21: PAPP-A giam, free beta-hCG tang
  • • T18: PAPP-A giam manh, beta-hCG giam
  • • T13: PAPP-A giam, beta-hCG giam
  • • Risk = base risk x LR(NT) x LR(PAPP-A) x LR(beta-hCG)

8. Han che quan trong cua NIPT (5 diem)

1. CHI SANG LOC, KHONG CHAN DOAN

Positive NIPT CAN diagnostic test truoc khi quyet dinh thai ky

2. Test failure ~2-5%

Thai som, fetal fraction thap, maternal obesity

3. False positive

Confined placental mosaicism, maternal CNV, vanishing twin, malignancy

4. False negative

Fetal fraction < 4%, mosaicism thap, BMI > 35

5. NIPT khong phat hien

Structural anomalies, NT day, pre-eclampsia markers, MC twins
