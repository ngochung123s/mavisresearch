14. Treatment of acute HF is based on diuretics for congestion, inotropes, and short-term MCS for peripheral hypoperfusion.
15. Patients hospitalized for HF should be carefully evaluated to exclude persistent signs of congestion. Oral treatment should beoptimized before discharge.
