1. ICSI la gi?

Dinh nghia

Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection - tiem truc tiep 1 tinh trung vao bao tuong noan (ooplasm) qua micropipette.

Lich su

Phat minh boi Palermo nam 1992. Ban dau chi cho severe male factor, nay chiem 70-80% cycle toan cau.

Bypass tu nhien

ICSI bypass hoan toan ZP va oolemma, khong can motility hay acrosome reaction binh thuong.

5. ICSI khong cai thien live birth (2 RCT 2024-2025)

Cung ket qua tu 2 chau luc: Trung Quoc (Lancet 2024) va Dan Mach (Nat Med 2025).

Wang 2024 + Berntsen 2025

  • • Wang 2024 (Lancet, n=2329): ICSI 33.8% vs c-IVF 36.6% LBR, RR 0.92 (0.83-1.03)
  • • Berntsen 2025 (Nat Med, n=822): ICSI 43.2% vs c-IVF 47.3% CLBR, RR 0.91 (0.79-1.06)
  • • Ket luan: c-IVF nen la first-line cho non-severe male factor
  • • ICSI danh rieng cho severe male factor

6. Sperm DNA damage - ICSI buffer tot hon IVF

Ribas-Maynou 2021 (Biol Rev, PMID 33644978) - meta 25,639 cycles.

Y nghia SDF

  • • SDF cao: giam RO o IVF, KHONG anh huong o ICSI
  • • ICSI bypass acrosome reaction, nen DNA damage it anh huong
  • • Chi dinh ICSI khi SDF > 30% (DFI)
