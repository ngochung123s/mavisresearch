NICE guideline review: fever in under 5s: assessment and initial management (NG143)
Arch Dis Child Educ Pract Ed. 2022. PMID: 34244233. DOI: 10.1136/archdischild-2020-320491.

Summary & Traffic Light System:
The National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) NG143 guideline provides a structured approach for the assessment and initial management of fever in children under 5 years of age.
The guideline utilizes a traffic light system to stratify the risk of serious bacterial illness into Green (low risk), Amber (intermediate risk), and Red (high risk). Non-blanching rash (petechiae and purpura) is classified as a RED flag feature mandating urgent hospital assessment and immediate antibiotic administration if meningococcal disease or septicaemia is suspected.
