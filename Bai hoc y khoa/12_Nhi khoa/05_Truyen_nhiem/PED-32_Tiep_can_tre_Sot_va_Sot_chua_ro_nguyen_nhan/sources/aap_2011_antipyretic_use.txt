American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) Clinical Report
Title: Fever and Antipyretic Use in Children
Authors: Janice E. Sullivan, Henry C. Farrar, Section on Clinical Pharmacology and Therapeutics, Committee on Drugs
Journal: Pediatrics. 2011;127(3):580-587. PMID: 21357332. DOI: 10.1542/peds.2010-3852.

Key Guidance:
1. Fever is not an illness but a physiologic mechanism that has beneficial effects in fighting infection.
2. The primary goal of treating the febrile child should be to improve the child's overall comfort rather than focus on the normalization of body temperature.
3. Healthcare providers should counsel parents that antipyretics do not prevent febrile seizures.
4. Combined or alternating antipyretic therapy with acetaminophen and ibuprofen may increase the risk of improper dosing and medication errors, and is not recommended as routine practice.
