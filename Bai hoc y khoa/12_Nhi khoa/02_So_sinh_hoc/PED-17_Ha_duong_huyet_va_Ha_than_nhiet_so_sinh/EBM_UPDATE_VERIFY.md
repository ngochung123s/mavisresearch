# PED-17 — Đối chiếu nguyên văn cho các ghi chú ⚖️ Cập nhật EBM

Deck PED-17 chỉ dạy theo barem (Giáo trình Nhi khoa Y Thái Bình, trang 46 – 52). EBM chỉ được thêm vào dưới dạng ghi chú ⚖️ ở những thẻ có nội dung barem đã lỗi thời. Mỗi ghi chú dưới đây kèm câu trích nguyên văn từ nguồn đã đọc (ngày 28/10/2026).

Nguồn:
- **AAP 2011**: Adamkin DH; Committee on Fetus and Newborn. *Postnatal glucose homeostasis in late-preterm and term infants.* Pediatrics 2011;127(3):575–9. PMID 21357346. Toàn văn đọc tại https://bchcicu.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Glucose-management.pdf (trang AAP bị chặn bởi Cloudflare).
- **PES 2015**: Thornton PS et al. *Recommendations from the Pediatric Endocrine Society for Evaluation and Management of Persistent Hypoglycemia in Neonates, Infants, and Children.* J Pediatr 2015;167(2):238–45. PMID 25957977. Toàn văn: Europe PMC PMC11891912.
- **Sugar Babies 2013**: Harris DL et al. *Dextrose gel for neonatal hypoglycaemia (the Sugar Babies Study).* Lancet 2013;382:2077–83. PMID 24075361. Abstract (evidence_bundle.json).

| Thẻ | Barem | Cập nhật | Trích nguyên văn |
|---|---|---|---|
| PED17-H0-01 | Một ngưỡng: ĐH < 45 mg/dL (2,6 mmol/L) | Không có một ngưỡng duy nhất; AAP xử trí trẻ nguy cơ theo giờ tuổi 25 / 35 mg/dL, trẻ có triệu chứng 40 mg/dL, đích > 45 mg/dL trước cữ bú | AAP 2011: "Current evidence does not support a specific concentration of glucose that can discriminate euglycemia from hypoglycemia" · "glucose concentrations of less than 25 mg/dL (birth to 4 hours of age) or less than 35 mg/dL (4–24 hours of age) be refed and that the glucose value be rechecked 1 hour after refeeding. Subsequent concentrations lower than 25 mg/dL, or lower than 35 mg/dL, respectively, after attempts to refeed, necessitate treatment with intravenous glucose." · "A reasonable (although arbitrary) cutoff for treating symptomatic infants is 40 mg/dL." · "The target glucose concentration is greater than 45 mg/dL before each feeding." |
| PED17-H3-01 | Sàng lọc ngay trong giờ đầu sau sinh | Cho bú trong giờ đầu rồi đo 30 phút sau cữ bú; non muộn/nhỏ so với tuổi thai đo trước mỗi cữ bú ≥ 24 giờ; không sàng lọc thường quy trẻ đủ tháng khỏe | AAP 2011: "At-risk infants should be fed by 1 hour of age and screened 30 minutes after the feeding." · "Neonatal glucose concentrations decrease after birth, to as low as 30 mg/dL during the first 1 to 2 hours after birth, and then increase" · "Late-preterm infants and infants who are small for gestational age should be fed every 2 to 3 hours and screened before each feeding for at least the first 24 hours." · "Routine screening and monitoring of blood glucose concentration is not needed in healthy term newborn infants after an entirely normal pregnancy and delivery." |
| PED17-H3-02 | Xét nghiệm nhanh: test Dextrostix | Que thử tại giường không đủ tin cậy làm phương pháp duy nhất; xác nhận bằng xét nghiệm enzym; không trì hoãn điều trị | AAP 2011: "the variation from the actual level may be as much as 10 to 20 mg/dL. Unfortunately, this variation is greatest at low glucose concentrations. There is no point-of-care method that is sufficiently reliable and accurate in the low range of blood glucose to allow it to be used as the sole method for screening for NH." · "laboratory enzymatic methods (eg, glucose oxidase, hexokinase, or dehydrogenase method)" · "Treatment of suspected NH should not be postponed while waiting for laboratory confirmation." PES 2015: "Point-of-care meters provide a convenient screening method for detecting hypoglycemia, but their accuracy is limited to approximately ±10–15 mg/dL (0.6–0.8 mmol/L) in the range of hypoglycemia." |
| PED17-H3-03 | Hạ ĐH dai dẳng: insulin đồng thời với glucose, cortisol | Mẫu máu then chốt lấy lúc đang hạ ĐH, trước điều trị | PES 2015: "Whenever possible, specimens (a "critical sample") for identifying the etiology of hypoglycemia should be obtained at the time of spontaneous presentation and before treatment that may rapidly alter the levels of BOHB, FFA, and insulin. Assays for PG, bicarbonate, BOHB, and lactate are readily available and useful for distinguishing categories of hypoglycemia disorders. Extra plasma can be held in reserve for specific tests (eg, plasma insulin, FFA, and C-peptide for suspected hyperinsulinism; plasma total and free carnitine and acyl-carnitine profile for a suspected disorder of fatty acid oxidation)." · "With few exceptions, blood tests performed while glucose concentrations are normal are not informative." |
| PED17-H4-06 | Đích ĐH > 2,5 mmol/l (> 3,5 mmol/l nếu cường insulin) | PES: > 50 mg/dL (< 48 giờ), > 60 mg/dL (> 48 giờ), > 70 mg/dL nếu nghi bệnh bẩm sinh | PES 2015: "3.1. For neonates with a suspected congenital hypoglycemia disorder … we recommend that the goal of treatment be to maintain a PG concentration >70 mg/dL (3.9 mmol/L). GRADE 1++00. 3.2. For high-risk neonates without a suspected congenital hypoglycemia disorder, we suggest the goal of treatment be to maintain a PG concentration >50 mg/dL (>2.8 mmol/L) for those aged <48 hours and >60 mg/dL (>3.3 mmol/L) for those aged >48 hours. GRADE 2+000." |
| PED17-H4-07 | Mức B: xem xét ăn dung dịch đường 40%, 200 mg/kg | Gel dextrose 40% 200 mg/kg là điều trị đầu tay được khuyên trong 48 giờ đầu | Sugar Babies 2013: "Babies aged 35-42 weeks' gestation, younger than 48-h-old, and at risk of hypoglycaemia were randomly assigned (1:1) … to 40% dextrose gel 200 mg/kg or placebo gel." · "Dextrose gel reduced the frequency of treatment failure compared with placebo (16 [14%] vs 29 [24%]; relative risk 0·57, 95% CI 0·33-0·98; p=0·04)." · "Dextrose gel should be considered for first-line treatment to manage hypoglycaemia in late preterm and term babies in the first 48 h after birth." |
