American Academy of Pediatrics Clinical Practice Guideline
Neurodiagnostic Evaluation of the Child With a Simple Febrile Seizure

Recommendation 1: A lumbar puncture is not routinely recommended in a well-appearing, fully immunized child who presents with a simple febrile seizure. Lumbar puncture should be performed when the child presents with meningeal signs or symptoms or when the history or examination suggests the presence of meningitis or intracranial infection. A lumbar puncture is an option when a child is considered underimmunized or when immunization status cannot be determined.

Recommendation 2: An electroencephalogram (EEG) should not be performed in the evaluation of a neurologically healthy child with a simple febrile seizure.

Recommendation 3: Neuroimaging (CT or MRI) should not be performed in the routine evaluation of a child with a simple febrile seizure.
