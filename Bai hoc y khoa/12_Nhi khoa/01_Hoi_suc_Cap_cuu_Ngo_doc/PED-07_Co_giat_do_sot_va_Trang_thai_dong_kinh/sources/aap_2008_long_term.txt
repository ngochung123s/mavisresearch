American Academy of Pediatrics Clinical Practice Guideline
Febrile Seizures: Clinical Practice Guideline for the Long-term Management of the Child With Simple Febrile Seizures

Recommendation: On the basis of the risks and benefits of the effective therapies, neither continuous nor intermittent anticonvulsant therapy with phenobarbital, valproic acid, carbamazepine, or phenytoin is recommended for children with one or more simple febrile seizures.
