American Epilepsy Society Guideline
Evidence-Based Guideline: Treatment of Convulsive Status Epilepticus in Children and Adults

Recommendation 1: Initial therapy phase (5 to 20 minutes): A benzodiazepine (specifically intramuscular midazolam 0.2 mg/kg, intravenous lorazepam 0.1 mg/kg, or intravenous diazepam 0.15 to 0.2 mg/kg) is recommended as the first-line treatment for convulsive status epilepticus. Time t1 at 5 minutes indicates when treatment should be initiated, whereas time t2 at 30 minutes indicates when neuronal injury may begin.
