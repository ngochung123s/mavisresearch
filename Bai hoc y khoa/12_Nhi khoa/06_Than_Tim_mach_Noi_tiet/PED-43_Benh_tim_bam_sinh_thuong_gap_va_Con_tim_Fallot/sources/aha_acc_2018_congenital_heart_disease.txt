2018 AHA/ACC Guideline for the Management of Adults With Congenital Heart Disease: A Report of the American College of Cardiology/American Heart Association Task Force on Clinical Practice Guidelines

Section 3.4 Tetralogy of Fallot:
Patients with repaired tetralogy of Fallot require lifelong follow-up with a congenital cardiologist because of late complications, including pulmonary regurgitation, right ventricular dysfunction, ventricular arrhythmias, and sudden cardiac death.
