IPNA clinical practice recommendations for the diagnosis and management of children with steroid-resistant nephrotic syndrome (Pediatr Nephrol 2020; 35:1529-1561. PMID: 32382828. DOI: 10.1007/s00467-020-04519-1)

Recommendation 1: Definition of steroid-resistant nephrotic syndrome:
We recommend defining SRNS as lack of complete remission of proteinuria after 4 weeks of daily prednisolone or prednisone at standard dose of 60 mg/m2/day or 2 mg/kg/day.

Recommendation 2: Calcineurin inhibitors as first-line therapy:
We recommend calcineurin inhibitors (cyclosporine A or tacrolimus) as first-line immunosuppressive therapy in children with SRNS without an identified genetic cause.
