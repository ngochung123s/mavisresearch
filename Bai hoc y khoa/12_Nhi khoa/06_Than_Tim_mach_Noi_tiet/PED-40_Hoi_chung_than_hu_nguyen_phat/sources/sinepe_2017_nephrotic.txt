# SINePe 2017 — PMID 28427453. Trích NGUYÊN VĂN từ toàn văn (truy xuất 2026-10-27).

[C-006] We suggest that PDN (60 mg/m2, maximum 60 mg) be given daily in a single dose or divided into 2 doses for 6 weeks, followed by a single dose of 40 mg/m2, maximum 40 mg PDN on alternate days for another 6 weeks, without any tapering of the dose.
