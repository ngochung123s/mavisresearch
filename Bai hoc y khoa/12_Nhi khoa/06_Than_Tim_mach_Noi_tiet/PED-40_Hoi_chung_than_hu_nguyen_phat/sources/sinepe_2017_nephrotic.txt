The Italian Society for Pediatric Nephrology (SINePe) consensus document on the management of nephrotic syndrome in children: Part I - Diagnosis and treatment of the first episode and the first relapse (Ital J Pediatr 2017; 43:41. PMID: 28427453. DOI: 10.1186/s13052-017-0356-x)

Consensus recommendation on initial presentation:
Initial treatment of pediatric nephrotic syndrome consists of daily prednisone at 60 mg/m2/day (maximum 60 mg/day) for 4 to 6 weeks followed by alternate-day prednisone at 40 mg/m2 (maximum 40 mg) for 4 to 6 weeks.
