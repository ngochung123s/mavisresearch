# KDIGO 2021 — PMID 34556256. Trích NGUYÊN VĂN từ toàn văn (truy xuất 2026-10-27).

[C-003] Recommendation 4.3.1.1: We recommend that oral glucocorticoids be given for 8 weeks (4 weeks of daily glucocorticoids followed by 4 weeks of alternate-day glucocorticoids) or 12 weeks (6 weeks of daily glucocorticoids followed by 6 weeks of alternate-day glucocorticoids) (1B).
