Secondary Prevention of Rheumatic Fever: A Scientific Statement From the American Heart Association. Intramuscular benzathine penicillin G is administered as a single dose of 1,200,000 units for patients weighing >= 27 kg (> 30 kg in pediatric textbooks) and 600,000 units for those weighing < 27 kg for eradication and secondary prophylaxis. Intramuscular benzathine penicillin G every 3 to 4 weeks (every 21 to 28 days) remains the gold standard for secondary prophylaxis. For patients with acute rheumatic fever without carditis, secondary prophylaxis is recommended for 5 years after the last attack or until age 21 years, whichever is longer. For patients with carditis but without residual heart disease, secondary prophylaxis is recommended for 10 years after the last attack or until age 21 years, whichever is longer. For patients with persistent valvular disease, secondary prophylaxis is recommended for 10 years after the last attack or until age 40 years (or lifelong), whichever is longer.
