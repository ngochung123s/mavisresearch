Revision of the Jones Criteria for the Diagnosis of Acute Rheumatic Fever in the Era of Doppler Echocardiography: A Scientific Statement From the American Heart Association. Low-risk populations are defined as those with ARF incidence <= 2 per 100,000 school-aged children per year or an all-age prevalence of rheumatic heart disease <= 1 per 1,000 population per year. Children not meeting these criteria are considered at moderate or high risk. In moderate- and high-risk populations, major joint manifestations include monoarthritis or polyarthritis and polyarthralgia; minor joint manifestations include monoarthralgia. In moderate- and high-risk populations, the fever cutoff as a minor criterion is >= 38.0 C, and the ESR cutoff is >= 30 mm/h. Subclinical carditis (carditis recognized on echocardiography/Doppler with no clinical murmur) should be included as a major criterion for carditis.
